Additionally, 57.88% of stocks are trading above their 200-day SMA. This longer-term indicator shows a more stable, though slightly less robust, positive momentum. Crossing above this average often indicates a long-term bullish trend, and with over half of the stocks above the line, it implies a moderate long-term positive sentiment.

Conclusion
