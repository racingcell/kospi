The 200-Day SMA is often regarded as a critical indicator for long-term trends, and a substantial percentage above this average can depict a robust upward trend for the broader market.

In summary, both short-term and long-term indicators suggest that the KOSPI is currently experiencing a healthy upward trend, reflecting overall positive market conditions. Investors may consider this a bullish signal but should also remain vigilant for any potential market corrections.
